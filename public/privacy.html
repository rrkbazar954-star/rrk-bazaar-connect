RRK Bazaar Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

RRK Bazaar is a marketplace app that helps customers discover products and connect with sellers.

Information We May Collect

Depending on the features you use, the app may process your name, phone number, email address, delivery address, location, account details, orders and seller or delivery-partner information.

How Information Is Used

Information may be used to provide app features, manage accounts and orders, help with deliveries, provide customer support, and protect the service.

Location

If you allow location access, your location may be used to help show nearby shops or delivery locations. You can manage location permission in your device settings.

Payments

If payment services are enabled, payment processing may be handled by the relevant payment provider. Do not share your UPI PIN or banking password with anyone.

Sharing of Information

Information may be shared with relevant buyers, sellers, delivery partners or service providers when needed to provide requested services. We do not promise that information is never shared.

Security and Retention

Reasonable measures should be used to protect information. Information may be retained as needed for service, legal and security purposes.

Your Choices

You can manage device permissions and contact the app operator to ask about your personal information or request corrections or deletion, subject to applicable law.

Children's Privacy

Parents or guardians should supervise children's use of the service.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact the RRK Bazaar app operator using the official support contact provided in the app.

This policy must be reviewed and updated to match the app's actual data practices before publication.
